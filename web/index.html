Which symbol does it trade?

XAUUSD exclusively. The strategy is not designed for other pairs or instruments.

Is it fully automated?

Yes. Once attached to a chart with your settings, it analyzes, enters, and exits trades without manual input.

Can I use it with my broker?

It works with most MT5 brokers. Low spread and fast execution on XAUUSD are recommended for best results.

Do I need a VPS?
